How I work

  • I test the standard fix before I trust the standard diagnosis — a fix that behaves as predicted is itself evidence, and one that doesn't is a bigger signal than the original number.
  • When a result looks too good, I treat it as a question, not a win — a perfect score is usually telling you where to look, not what to report.
  • I say what I don't know as directly as what I do. A hedged, honest result holds up better than a confident number that can't survive a second look.
  • I check the notebook, not the narrative — real executed output, not a description of what the output should show.
